Mission Objectives

Why Europa?

Europa is believed to contain a massive liquid-water ocean beneath its frozen crust, making it one of the most promising locations in our solar system to search for life.


Primary Objectives

  • Search for biosignatures.
  • Study Europa's subsurface ocean.
  • Test deep-space living technologies.
  • Prepare for future human settlements.
